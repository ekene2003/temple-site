About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Oil & Gas Logistics and Engineering

Falcon Point Service Limited is a Nigerian-based company delivering a full suite of oil and gas support services, with a strong presence across West Africa. We specialize in vessel management, offshore logistics, civil and engineering works, and global cargo transport.

Our team combines extensive experience with a deep understanding of both international standards and local operating conditions. Whether managing complex offshore operations or ensuring timely cargo delivery, we are committed to achieving superior outcomes safely, efficiently, and in full regulatory compliance.

We pride ourselves on our operational integrity, commitment to safety, and a hands-on approach that ensures every project meets client expectations and industry benchmarks.
