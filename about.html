About Falcon Point Servicess

Falcon Point Services is a trusted provider of oil and gas logistics, offshore support, vessel management, and engineering solutions across West Africa. We combine deep industry knowledge with technical expertise to deliver reliable, compliant, and safety-driven operations for clients in the energy, maritime, and industrial sectors.

With years of experience in pressure testing, leak detection, cargo transport, and vessel operations, our team ensures every project is handled with precision and professionalism. We understand the challenges of operating in complex offshore and onshore environments, and we have the capability to respond quickly and effectively—without compromising quality or safety.

Driven by Purpose, Committed to Excellence

Our Vision

To be exceptional not just as a company but as a service partner of choice for all our existing and potential customers in the Oil and Gas Industry through premium quality service delivery.

Our Mission

We are committed to empowering and creating shared values by investing and growing in the regions in which we live and operate.

Our Commitment to Safety and the Environment

At Falcon Point, safety is non-negotiable. We follow strict Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) protocols, conducting regular risk assessments and ensuring every team member is trained to meet global safety standards. Our goal is to protect human life, preserve the environment, and ensure responsible, sustainable operations.
